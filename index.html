The Four Stages of Consciousness Quiz

Find out which stage you live in: To Me, By Me, Through Me, or As Me.

Every person moves through four stages of consciousness. Each stage has its own relationship to power. In Stage 1, life happens to you. In Stage 2, life happens by you. In Stage 3, life happens through you. In Stage 4, life happens as you. Most people spend years in one stage before they even know the map exists.

Stage 1 is To Me. This is the Victim stage. The world acts and you react. Blame, comparison, and waiting for rescue live here. Stage 2 is By Me. This is the Creator stage. You take the wheel, set goals, and build outcomes through effort and will. Stage 3 is Through Me. This is the Witness stage. You stop gripping the wheel so hard and start noticing that something larger moves through your actions. Stage 4 is As Me. This is the Unity stage. The separation between you and the flow of life closes. There is no longer a doer and a done-to. There is only what is happening, and you are it.

This quiz places you inside the map right now, not where you wish you were. Answer honestly. The result names your current stage, the pattern that keeps you there, and the next move.
